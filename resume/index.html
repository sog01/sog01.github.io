About me

I am a Lead AI Platform Engineer and Architect with 10+ years of software and platform engineering experience and, for the last two years, a track record of shipping production agentic AI for enterprise and government clients in the United Arab Emirates, including Dubai Government entities, as part of a Dubai-based team that I work with remotely from Indonesia. I design and build the foundations that let AI agents operate against enterprise systems of record: multi-agent orchestration with MCP tool calling, retrieval and evaluation pipelines, guardrails and audit trails, least-privilege identity, and deployment patterns for on-premises and air-gapped environments where data cannot leave the customer's network.

I have shipped production agent and LLM workloads on Google Vertex AI and Amazon Bedrock, delivered a transactional chatbot on Azure OpenAI / AI Foundry, and served models on-premises with vLLM and Ollama, designing model-agnostic gateways so customers can bring their own model. On the enterprise side I have integrated an ERP end to end: Oracle NetSuite (SuiteTalk REST) for sales orders, invoices and customer payments, feeding a finance reconciliation platform that cut manual reconciliation by 90%.

My foundation was built at Indonesia's leading technology companies (Tokopedia, Ruangguru, Sorabel, Evermos), where I designed company-wide platforms for change data capture, search, messaging, CRM and role-based access control that raised engineering productivity by 70–90%. I build and operate end-to-end MLOps pipelines with Kubeflow, KServe, MLflow, vLLM and Ollama; use Qdrant and pgvector for retrieval-augmented generation; and run evaluation, tracing and monitoring with Langfuse and Arize Phoenix.

I am proficient in Python, Go, Java, JavaScript, PHP and C, with extensive experience in PostgreSQL, MySQL, BigQuery, Redis, Elasticsearch and Kafka, advanced Kubernetes (Helm, Kustomize) and Terraform, and cloud experience across Google Cloud, AWS and Azure. I speak at engineering events on agentic harness design: the tools, memory, control flow and evaluation that turn LLMs into reliable production systems.
